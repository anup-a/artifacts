What readers need

Nothing. A reader opens the link, reads the page and comments under a name they type. No account, no email, no install.

Questions

Will it stay free?

It is free today and we have no paid plan. We cannot promise it stays free forever. If the limits or the price change, we give at least 30 days' notice before any change that affects pages you have already published.

What happens when I hit a limit?

The publish is refused with a clear error, and your existing pages keep working. Delete pages you no longer need, or write to us.
